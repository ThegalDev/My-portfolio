01 / CONTEXT

The problem

Candidate outreach creates several connected tasks: reaching someone, handling availability, recording an outcome, scheduling a meeting, and following up when the candidate is busy or unavailable.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My contribution

I built the voice interview scheduler using n8n, Vapi, Twilio, Airtable, Google Calendar, OpenAI, and Gmail integrations, as documented in my CV.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION

What is actually built

The recording shows an n8n workflow that retrieves pending candidates, processes each candidate, makes a call, waits for a result, retrieves call status, and routes the outcome. Visible branches include calendar and message steps, a confirmed-state update, a retry counter, and a failed/retry state.

04 / DECISIONS

Why this approach

Treat a call outcome as a state transition, rather than a one-off message. Confirmed, busy, and retry paths need different follow-up. Keep the candidate record and scheduling actions connected so the workflow can continue from the correct state.

05 / EVIDENCE

What you can verify

A public 1:23 screen recording shows the actual workflow canvas. The CV describes natural-language voice handling and candidate status tracking. The recording is evidence of the build; it is not evidence of production adoption, candidate satisfaction, or quantified savings.
