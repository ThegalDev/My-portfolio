01 / CONTEXT

The challenge

Recruiters pulled data by hand across Workable, Google Forms, HireFlix, and ClickUp. A nine-stage hiring pipeline lacked centralised reporting and consistent scoring.

02 / MY ROLE

What I owned

I owned full-cycle recruitment and built the AI-assisted Recruitment Operating System, partnering with department heads on hiring requirements, success criteria, and role-specific assessments.

03 / JUDGEMENT

The decisions behind it

Use structured scorecards and rule-based stage movement for consistency. Automate coordination and summarisation, while preserving human approval at every subjective stage.

04 / DELIVERY

How it came together

Designed an event-driven architecture orchestrated in n8n: data ingestion, AI scoring and summaries, digest notifications, candidate-report generation, and global error handling. A technical handover specified workflow logic, database schema, API mapping, and phased rollout.

05 / EVIDENCE

What changed

Reduced screening time and notification fatigue, standardised scorecards, real-time analytics, and audit trails. These are qualitative outcomes.
