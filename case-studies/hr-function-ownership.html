01 / CONTEXT

The challenge

A growing HR function needed consistent personnel records, candidate communication, and employee lifecycle administration without investing in a conventional ATS.

02 / MY ROLE

What I owned

I independently managed the HR function across recruitment, onboarding, payroll, employee relations, learning and development, engagement, performance administration, and offboarding.

03 / JUDGEMENT

The decisions behind it

Use a lightweight Notion system to centralise records and Make for candidate acknowledgements. Support the technology with policies, SOPs, and standardised documentation.

04 / DELIVERY

How it came together

Built the Notion HR management system and low-code recruitment communication workflow. Partnered with managers on workforce requirements and hiring priorities, managed payroll and lifecycle administration, and supported expatriate employees with onboarding and documentation.

05 / EVIDENCE

What changed

Centralised personnel information, more consistent candidate acknowledgements, and standardised HR documentation. Improved record management and process consistency are qualitative outcomes.

Notion · Make · Policies · SOPs · Lifecycle administration
