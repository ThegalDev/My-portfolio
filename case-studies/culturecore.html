01 / CONTEXT

The problem

A company can name its values without making them useful in everyday communication. This prototype explores how a culture archetype can become a practical writing standard.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My contribution

I built Culturecore using Next.js, TypeScript, React, and AI-assisted development. The public repository provides the application source.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION

What is actually built

The public Voice Architect lets visitors choose from 15 culture archetypes, then generates personality guidance, emotional pillars, vocabulary to use and avoid, and an internal email example. I reviewed the live Warm & Family-Oriented flow and the matching TypeScript generator.

04 / DECISIONS

Why this approach

Make an abstract culture choice concrete: words, guidance, and a sample message. The current generator uses archetype-based rules and templates. The public interface also includes Document Studio and settings; the verified flow here is Voice Architect.

05 / EVIDENCE

What you can verify

The live prototype produced a warm voice standard after selecting an archetype. The repository exposes the generator implementation. This is presented as a prototype—not evidence of enterprise customers, a live AI inference service, or measured culture outcomes.
