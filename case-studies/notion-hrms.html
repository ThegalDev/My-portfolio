HR SYSTEMS / PUBLIC TEMPLATE

A lean HR system for small teams

Employee records, attendance, and leave balances connected in one Notion workspace. Built from the realities of being the only HR person in a startup.

Published template + implementation guide

01 / CONTEXT

The problem

A small team still creates a large coordination burden for a solo HR practitioner: personnel records, attendance, leave entitlements, payroll inputs, and employee support. My published guide explains why I chose a lightweight workspace rather than another fragmented spreadsheet.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My contribution

I designed and built the Notion template, its linked databases and formulas, and the public implementation guide. My CV also documents a lightweight Notion HR system built while independently managing HR at GT Powertank.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION

What is actually built

The employee database contains more than 30 properties and views for active, inactive, and probation staff. Attendance records link back to each employee. Leave records track entitlement, usage, and remaining balance through relations, rollups, and formulas. The public product includes a quick-start guide and onboarding checklist.

04 / DECISIONS

Why this approach

Keep the employee record at the centre, and connect other activities to it. Make entitlement configurable rather than burying a fixed policy in a formula. Design the workflow for solo HR practitioners and founders who need an approachable starting point.

05 / EVIDENCE

What you can verify

The published article explains the database structure and shows worked examples, including a ten-day entitlement with four days taken and six remaining. The template is listed publicly on Gumroad and Selar. No adoption count or measured time saving is claimed here.
