01 / CONTEXT

The challenge

Performance signals were scattered across tools, making it harder to identify concerns early and consistently.

02 / MY ROLE

What I owned

I independently owned the performance management cycle and designed the Performance Intelligence framework, partnering with department heads to turn job descriptions into measurable KPIs.

03 / JUDGEMENT

The decisions behind it

Connect ongoing operational signals to structured daily and weekly reporting. Pair monitoring with check-ins, employee self-assessment, manager evaluation, and KPI realignment; preserve human decision boundaries.

04 / DELIVERY

How it came together

Integrated task progression, responsiveness, time tracking, and process compliance into reporting. The framework includes four-tier risk scoring, timezone-aware check-ins, leadership digests, and structured review points at 90 days and six months.

05 / EVIDENCE

What changed

Earlier identification of performance risks and evidence-based manager intervention and recognition. These are qualitative outcomes.

What the signals can—and cannot—tell us

Operational signals are prompts for a conversation, not a complete assessment of a person. This framework includes timezone-aware check-ins and human decision boundaries. Role expectations, self-assessment, and manager review form part of the wider performance cycle.

A responsible interpretation also needs to consider role differences, working arrangements, and individual circumstances. The illustration describes the framework at a high level and includes no employee data or scoring thresholds.
